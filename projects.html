📁 4 Benchmarked Shadow Projects — MBA Sem 1

Each project uses a 4-phase framework: Company Context → Manual Framework → Data & Analysis → AI Lab. Students must complete and submit the manual phases before the AI phase unlocks each week.

P1: Netflix/Jio P2: Zomato/Swiggy P3: Startup BMC P4: KPI Dashboard

🎬 P1: Market Entry Snapshot Netflix / Jio Weeks 1–6 (9 hours)

Objective: Analyse one city/region for product or service expansion using pricing, competitors, and customer segments.

AI Tools: Google Trends · ChatGPT · Excel AI · Power BI/Looker Studio · Beautiful.ai/Tome

📋 4-Phase Framework

🟠 Orange = Manual (no AI) · 🤖 Green = AI Augmentation · Complete manual phases before proceeding to AI phase.

🔵 Phase 1 — Company Context (Hours 1–3)
  • Select Netflix or Jio as your benchmarked company
  • Study company profile: revenue model, pricing tiers, expansion strategy
  • Select ONE city (5–20 lakh population) for your market entry analysis
  • Create a 1-page company snapshot (Word/Docs) — NO AI yet
  • Deliverable: Company profile note + city selection rationale (200 words)
🟠 Phase 2 — Manual Framework (Hours 4–6)
  • Apply Market Segmentation Canvas OR Porter's Five Forces for your city — pen/paper or whiteboard
  • Manually calculate estimated TAM (Total Addressable Market) for your segment
  • Write 2–3 hypotheses in format: 'I believe [segment] in [city] will adopt [company] if [condition] because [reason]'
  • Collect data from: census.gov.in, TRAI reports, company website, Google Trends
  • Deliverable: Completed manual worksheet + data checklist (Excel template provided below)
🟢 Phase 3 — Data Structuring (Hours 7–8)
  • Build Excel workbook with 3 sheets: City Demographics, Market Analysis, Google Trends Data
  • Calculate manually: TAM, market penetration %, competitor market share
  • Write a 300-word 'Before AI Insight Note' summarising your manual findings and what you want AI to validate
  • Deliverable: Excel workbook + Before AI note submitted to LMS
🤖 Phase 4 — AI Lab (Hours 8–9)
  • Tool 1 — Google Trends: Compare search interest for Netflix/Jio in your city vs competitors (12-month trend)
  • Tool 2 — ChatGPT: PESTLE analysis prompt → 'Act as a strategic analyst. Conduct PESTLE for OTT/Telecom in [city] India 2026. Focus on Tier-2 city factors. Present as structured table.'
  • Tool 3 — Excel AI: Generate competitive comparison table and subscriber growth forecast
  • Tool 4 — Power BI / Looker Studio: Build 4-KPI dashboard (TAM, Market Share, Google Trend Index, Revenue Projection)
  • Tool 5 — Beautiful.ai / Tome: Create 6-8 slide executive presentation
  • Write 1-page AI Reflection Note: What did AI find that you missed? Where did human judgment matter?
  • Deliverable: Dashboard + Presentation + AI Reflection Note + Viva (5 min presentation + 3 min Q&A)

📊 Evaluation Rubric

CriteriaWeightFocus
Company Understanding30%Business model, pricing, competitive landscape clarity
Concept Application30%Framework quality, hypothesis testing, manual analysis accuracy
AI Tool Proficiency40%Prompt engineering, dashboard quality, AI vs human insight balance

🍔 P2: Customer Journey Mapping Zomato / Swiggy Weeks 7–8 (9 hours)

Objective: Map the end-to-end customer journey (order to delivery) and identify 3 friction points with solutions.

AI Tools: ChatGPT · Excel AI · Canva / Miro · Power BI

📋 4-Phase Framework

🟠 Orange = Manual (no AI) · 🤖 Green = AI Augmentation · Complete manual phases before proceeding to AI phase.

🔵 Phase 1 — Ecosystem Understanding (Hours 1–3)
  • Study Zomato or Swiggy: business model, app flow, delivery ecosystem, revenue streams
  • Download and use the app actively — note every screen, wait time, and interaction
  • Read 20+ app store reviews and identify recurring complaints
  • Deliverable: 1-page ecosystem overview + app flow sketch (hand-drawn)
🟠 Phase 2 — Manual Journey Map (Hours 4–6)
  • Map 7–9 touchpoints from: App open → Search → Select → Order → Payment → Tracking → Delivery → Post-delivery
  • For each touchpoint: note Customer Action | Emotion (😊😐😟) | Pain Point
  • Identify 3 friction points by frequency (from app reviews) and severity
  • Deliverable: Completed journey map table (Excel or hand-drawn) + friction point summary
🟢 Phase 3 — Data Analysis (Hours 7–8)
  • Categorise all friction points: Technology | Operations | Communication
  • Build an issue-frequency Excel table: Friction Point | No. of Reviews Mentioning | Severity (1–5)
  • Write Before AI note (200 words): What are your top 3 insights from manual analysis?
🤖 Phase 4 — AI Lab (Hours 8–9)
  • ChatGPT Prompt: 'Act as a service design expert. For Zomato/Swiggy in [city], map the complete customer journey from app open to post-delivery. For each touchpoint, identify emotion, pain point, and solution. Present as a detailed table.'
  • Excel AI: Build issue-frequency mapping chart — friction point vs. frequency vs. severity
  • ChatGPT: 'Based on this complaint data [paste], identify top 3 friction points by frequency and severity. For each, recommend a specific operational or tech solution.'
  • Canva AI / Miro: Build visual journey map (swim-lane style)
  • Deliverable: Visual journey map + friction point analysis report + AI Reflection Note + Viva

📊 Evaluation Rubric

CriteriaWeightFocus
Journey Completeness30%All 7–9 touchpoints covered with emotion mapping
Friction Analysis30%Quality of 3 friction points — evidence-based, categorised
AI Augmentation40%Prompt quality, visual output, Before vs After AI comparison

🚀 P3: Startup Business Model Study Google for Startups / Microsoft Accelerator Weeks 9–10 (8 hours)

Objective: Analyse revenue model, cost structure, and value proposition of a live or recently funded startup.

AI Tools: Notion AI · ChatGPT · Crunchbase · Excel AI

📋 4-Phase Framework

🟠 Orange = Manual (no AI) · 🤖 Green = AI Augmentation · Complete manual phases before proceeding to AI phase.

🔵 Phase 1 — Startup Selection & Context (Hours 1–3)
  • Choose ONE startup from Google for Startups India portfolio OR Microsoft for Startups portfolio
  • Research: funding stage, founding year, product/service, target market, geography
  • Sources: Crunchbase, Inc42, YourStory, startup's own website
  • Deliverable: 1-page startup profile (no AI)
🟠 Phase 2 — Manual Business Model Canvas (Hours 4–5)
  • Fill all 9 BMC blocks manually: Key Partners | Key Activities | Key Resources | Value Propositions | Customer Relationships | Channels | Customer Segments | Cost Structure | Revenue Streams
  • Calculate approximate: Revenue estimate (from news/public data), Top 2 cost heads, Break-even hypothesis
  • Deliverable: Completed BMC (printed template or whiteboard photo)
🟢 Phase 3 — Viability Assessment (Hours 6–7)
  • Score the startup on: Market Size (1–5) | Business Model Clarity (1–5) | Competitive Moat (1–5) | Scalability (1–5) | Funding Stage Alignment (1–5)
  • Write Before AI viability note (200 words): Is this startup viable? Why / Why not?
🤖 Phase 4 — AI Lab (Hours 7–8)
  • Notion AI: Build structured Business Model Canvas with AI suggestions for each block
  • ChatGPT: 'Act as a venture capital analyst. Analyse this startup [paste summary]. Evaluate: revenue model viability, cost structure risks, competitive moat, scalability. Provide a viability score 1–10 with reasoning.'
  • ChatGPT: 'Create a comprehensive BMC for [startup name]. Fill all 9 blocks. Present in structured format.'
  • Excel AI: Build financial model — Revenue vs Cost scenarios (3 scenarios: conservative, base, optimistic)
  • Deliverable: AI-enhanced BMC + viability report + financial model + Viva

📊 Evaluation Rubric

CriteriaWeightFocus
Startup Understanding30%Accuracy of business model, funding, market context
BMC Quality30%Completeness and accuracy of all 9 blocks
AI Viability Analysis40%Prompt quality, financial modelling, critical assessment vs AI output

📊 P4: Functional Dashboard Creation Microsoft / Google Weeks 11–12 (8 hours)

Objective: Build a 3–4 KPI dashboard (sales, customers, costs) using structured data with AI insights.

AI Tools: Power BI / Looker Studio · Excel AI · ChatGPT · Copilot

📋 4-Phase Framework

🟠 Orange = Manual (no AI) · 🤖 Green = AI Augmentation · Complete manual phases before proceeding to AI phase.

🔵 Phase 1 — KPI Design (Hours 1–3)
  • Choose one business function: Sales OR Marketing OR Operations OR HR
  • Study 2 public Microsoft or Google dashboards (annual reports, investor decks)
  • Identify 3–4 SMART KPIs for your function (e.g., Revenue Growth %, Customer Acquisition Cost, On-Time Delivery %)
  • Sketch the dashboard layout on paper — which charts for which KPIs
  • Deliverable: KPI specification sheet (template provided below)
🟠 Phase 2 — Manual Data Work (Hours 4–5)
  • Faculty provides a structured dataset (Excel) — clean and organise it
  • Build manual pivot tables for each KPI
  • Calculate variance: Actual vs Plan for each KPI
  • Write Before AI note: What story does your manual data tell?
🟢 Phase 3 — Dashboard Structure (Hours 6–7)
  • Build the dashboard skeleton in Excel (no AI features yet) — bar charts, line charts, KPI cards
  • Validate all calculations manually before moving to AI tools
🤖 Phase 4 — AI Lab (Hours 7–8)
  • Excel AI / Copilot: 'Analyse this sales data [paste]. Recommend 4 KPIs, identify the biggest variance driver, and suggest 2 actions.'
  • Power BI / Looker Studio: Import cleaned data, apply AI insights panel, enable natural language Q&A
  • ChatGPT: 'Act as a BI analyst. For this data [paste summary], generate a narrative insight paragraph for each of the 4 KPIs as it would appear on an executive dashboard.'
  • Final dashboard must include: 2 charts + 4 KPI cards + 1 AI-generated insight summary
  • Deliverable: Power BI / Looker Studio file (.pbix or shareable link) + insight report + Viva

📊 Evaluation Rubric

CriteriaWeightFocus
KPI Relevance & Design30%SMART KPIs, alignment to chosen function
Data Accuracy30%Clean data, correct calculations, manual validation
Dashboard Quality40%Visual clarity, AI insights, narrative quality, interactivity
